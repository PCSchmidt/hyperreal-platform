# ADR 0002: Licensing

**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-30)
**Decision:** Code under Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE`). Documentation, specifications and benchmark data under CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-DOCS`). Third-party assets, engine content and model weights are excluded and individually recorded; weights carry their own model-card license.
**Rationale:** Permissive, includes a patent grant, credible to employers, keeps the author's option to relicense or commercialise future versions. Not legal advice.
